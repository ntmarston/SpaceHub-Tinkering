
System of equations:
Equation I
\begin{align*}
\frac{k T_c}{\mu m_p} + \frac{4\sigma T_c^4}{3c\rho}-\left[\frac{\dot{M}\Omega^2f^4}{3\pi\alpha\rho}\right]^{2/3}=0
\end{align*}

Equation II
\begin{align*}
\rho^{2/3}(\kappa_0+\kappa_1\rho T_c^{-7/2})-\left[\frac{32\pi\sigma\Omega R^3}{9GM\dot{M}f^4}\right]T_c^4\left[\frac{3\pi\alpha}{\dot{M}f^4\Omega^2}\right]^{1/3}=0
\end{align*}

Definitions:
\begin{align*}
f^4=1-\sqrt{\frac{R_*}{R}}
\end{align*}
\begin{align*}
\Omega=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{R^3}}
\end{align*}

Known constants:
\begin{align*}
    \dot{M}, R, M, \alpha, \kappa_0, \kappa_1, \sigma, G, c, k, \mu, m_p
\end{align*}


Full system of equations (Shakura-Sunyaev, $Q\gtrsim 1$):

Note: Assumes Gaussian vertical density profile $\rho(z) = \rho_c \exp(-z^2/2H^2)$

\[
\begin{array}{l}
1.\ \Sigma \approx \sqrt{2\pi}\rho_c H; \quad (\text{$\rho_c$ is midplane density}) \\
2.\ H = c_s \Omega^{-1}; \quad (\text{scale height parameter}) \\
3.\ c_s^2 = P/\rho_c; \\
4.\ P = \frac{\rho_c k T_c}{\mu m_p} + \frac{4\sigma T_c^4}{3c}; \\
5.\ \frac{4\sigma T_c^4}{3\tau} = \frac{3GM\dot{M}}{8\pi R^3} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac{R_*}{R}\right)^{1/2} \right]; \\
6.\ \tau = \Sigma (\kappa_0 + \kappa_1 \rho_c T_c^{-7/2}) \\
7.\ \nu\Sigma = \frac{\dot{M}}{3\pi} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac{R_*}{R}\right)^{1/2} \right]; \\
8.\ \nu = \alpha c_s H;
\end{array}
\]

Full system of equations (Sirko \& Goodman, $Q \approx Q_{min}$):

Note: Assumes UNIFORM vertical density $\rho(z) = \rho$ (slab model), so $\Sigma = 2\rho H$

\[
\begin{array}{l}
1.\ \Sigma = 2\rho H; \quad (\text{uniform slab, not Gaussian}) \\
2.\ H = c_s \Omega^{-1}; \quad (\text{scale height parameter}) \\
3.\ c_s^2 = P/\rho; \\
4.\ P = \frac{\rho k T_c}{\mu m_p} + \frac{4\sigma T_c^4}{3c}; \\
5.\ \rho = \frac{\Omega^2}{2\pi G Q_{min}}; \quad (\text{from Q condition}) \\
6.\ \nu\Sigma = \frac{\dot{M}}{3\pi} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac{R_*}{R}\right)^{1/2} \right]; \\
7.\ \nu = \alpha c_s H;
\end{array}
\]

\textit{Key difference from Shakura-Sunyaev:} The $\rho$ in the Sirko \& Goodman regime is a uniform
volume density (not a midplane density from a Gaussian profile), which causes a factor of $\sim 1.25$
discontinuity in $\rho$ at the $Q = Q_{min}$ transition. However, $H$ and $c_s$ remain continuous,
and the viscosity $\nu = \alpha c_s H$ is well-defined in both regimes.
